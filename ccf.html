About the CCF

The Country Capacity Framework (CCF) describes three indicative country capacity models for pathogen genomic surveillance (PGS) — Hybrid domestic, Integrated domestic and Regional/global support. For each model, capacities are described across four categories and 14 indicators. The models are indicative: a country may align with different models across different indicators.

Countries can use the CCF to conduct a situation analysis, plan the development of core capacities, identify gaps or opportunities for the next best investment, and cost and prioritize investments.

How to self-assess: for each indicator, click the description that best matches your current capacity (or “Not yet in place” if none apply). The panel shows the closest overall model, a profile by category, and the next-step capacity for each indicator. Your answers are saved in this browser only.
